# Market Revolution | Clean Blog archive edition

## Opening
Unzip, then open `index.html`. The 8 HTML pages and their existing Clean Blog navigation remain intact. The original public Start Bootstrap Clean Blog stylesheet/Bootstrap JS and some archival previews require an internet connection.

## New in this edition
16 additional archive image placements (two per page), arranged like a small document exhibition rather than a generic card dashboard. Visual captions include titles, dates (or uncertainty), institutional attribution, and original collection links. Existing timeline, before/after, transportation schematic, primary source sections, scroll animation and dark mode remain.

## Before handing in
1. Open every page in a browser. Ensure the archival previews actually load. Image URLs are external and may be blocked; an original catalog link appears if loading fails. Some historical image dates are uncertain or later than 1850, and captions disclose this. You can download images locally from the linked records after checking reuse rights and replace each `<img src>` with a local `assets/images/` path.
2. Review linked catalog records; verify displayed images correspond to the correct records. Treat any historical photograph taken after 1850 as a retrospective visual, not an eyewitness image from the Market Revolution.
3. Write your own historical analysis and Final Verdict, and fill the Process Paper in your team's own words. The class guide restricts AI-written historical analysis and requires meaningful AI assistance to be disclosed in the process paper/bibliography.
4. Check the 1,000–1,200 student-written words, 6–8 sources, at least two primary and three secondary sources, source attribution on images, and 300–400-word Process Paper requirements.
5. Optional: avoid CDN dependency by downloading the published Clean Blog stylesheet and scripts within their license terms.

### Image rendering fix
Original archive thumbnails are remote from Library of Congress and may be blocked or broken.
When they cannot be loaded, the website now shows a source-title tile linking to the official document instead of repeated error notices. These tiles are NOT historical images.
For fully offline original historical images, download them from each linked institution and replace the `src` with a local file in `assets/images/`, while keeping credit and original source links. Do not misrepresent modern photographs as 1830s photographs.
