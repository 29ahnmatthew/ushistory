The Market Revolution

Innovation in History: Impact, Influence, Change

Historical Question

How did the market revolution transform American lifestyle and economy?

Our Working Thesis

The Market Revolution transformed American life and the economy by enabling urban migration, creating more specialized jobs, and shifting people from self-sufficient farming to a market economy based on growing transportation, factories, a national market, and wage labor.
