Short- and Long-Term Effects

Immediate shifts and lasting consequences

Short-term

Transportation became faster and cheaper, factories could produce more products , and people moved to cities for higher salary jobs.

Long-term

The Market Revolution allowed to create a national economy and promoted industrialization and urban growth.

Unintended Consequences

Unexpectedly, the Market Revolution had drawbacks as well. It increased inequality because some factory workers received low wages and worked in poor environments. The growth of the cotton economy also increased demand for slavery in the South.
